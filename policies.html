Privacy Policy

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we understand that your medical information is deeply personal. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality of your medical records.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, contact details, medical history, biopsy reports, radiology scans (CDs/films), and other health-related data necessary for your diagnosis and treatment.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide accurate medical diagnosis and treatment planning.
  • To coordinate with other medical professionals (e.g., medical oncologists, pathologists) involved in your care.
  • To process payments and coordinate with your health insurance provider (if applicable).

3. AI-Assisted Diagnosis & Data Security

How AI uses your data: Our AI tools analyze your anonymized medical data to cross-reference global research databases. The AI does not store your personal identity. All final medical decisions are made by Dr. Khadkaban.

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Medical Disclaimer

The information provided on the Phoenix Cancer Clinic website is for general educational and informational purposes only. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Always seek the advice of a qualified physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read on this website.

While we utilize Advanced AI-Assisted Diagnosis to empower our medical team, AI is a supportive tool and does not replace the clinical judgment, expertise, or experience of a human doctor. All treatment plans and surgical decisions are made solely by Dr. Khadkaban.

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call our clinic immediately or go to the nearest emergency room.

Insurance & Billing

We believe in transparent and ethical billing. Cancer treatment is a significant investment in your health, and we want to ensure there are no hidden surprises.

Cashless Treatment & Insurance

Phoenix Cancer Clinic works with leading health insurance providers and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) to facilitate cashless hospitalization for eligible patients.

  • Please bring your health insurance card and policy details during your first consultation.
  • Our administrative team will help you verify your coverage and obtain pre-authorization for surgeries.
  • We also assist patients eligible for government schemes (such as Ayushman Bharat) where applicable.

Payment Options

For treatments not covered by insurance, we offer flexible payment options. We accept cash, credit/debit cards, UPI, and bank transfers.

Cost Estimates

Before any major surgery or treatment plan begins, Dr. Khadkaban and our billing team will provide you with a clear, written estimate of the expected costs. If your treatment plan changes, we will update you promptly.
