Lung & Chest Cancer

Thoracic Oncology Care

Lung and chest cancers (Thoracic Oncology) require highly specialized surgical skills. At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, Dr. Khadkaban is experienced in performing complex thoracic procedures with a primary focus on minimizing recovery time, preserving lung function, and ensuring complete tumor removal.

We utilize advanced imaging and AI-assisted planning to precisely map the tumor before surgery, ensuring maximum safety and efficacy during the operation.

Signs, Symptoms & Risk Factors

Early detection significantly improves outcomes

When to see a doctor:

  • A cough that doesn't go away or gets worse over time.
  • Coughing up blood or rust-colored sputum.
  • Chest pain that is often worse when you breathe deeply or cough.
  • Shortness of breath or wheezing.
  • Unexplained weight loss and loss of appetite.
  • Hoarseness or recurring respiratory infections (like bronchitis).

Major Risk Factors:

  • Smoking: The leading cause. This includes cigarettes, cigars, and pipes.
  • Secondhand Smoke: Exposure to smoke from others.
  • Environmental Toxins: Exposure to asbestos, radon gas, or air pollution.
  • Family History: A genetic predisposition to lung cancer.

Our Advanced Surgeries

Complex thoracic procedures performed with precision

Lobectomy

The most common surgery for early-stage lung cancer. It involves the removal of the lobe of the lung containing the tumor. The remaining lobes in that lung will expand to fill the space, allowing you to continue breathing normally.

Pneumonectomy

Reserved for cases of advanced localized cancer where the tumor is too large or centrally located. This procedure involves the removal of an entire lung. Patients can still lead active lives with one healthy lung, supported by our comprehensive rehabilitation team.

Oesophagectomy

Surgical removal of part or all of the esophagus. This is a highly complex procedure often performed for esophageal cancer (cancer of the food pipe). It requires expert surgical skill to reconstruct the digestive tract and restore the ability to swallow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common concerns

Will I be able to breathe normally after lung surgery?

Yes. After a Lobectomy, the remaining lobes expand to compensate, and most patients regain normal breathing. After a Pneumonectomy, you can live a normal, active life with one lung, though you may experience shortness of breath during heavy exertion. We provide pulmonary rehabilitation to help you adapt.

What is the recovery time for thoracic surgery?

Hospital stays typically range from 3 to 7 days depending on the procedure. Full recovery at home can take 4 to 8 weeks. We will provide a detailed post-operative care plan, including breathing exercises and pain management, to ensure a smooth recovery.

Is minimally invasive (VATS) surgery an option?

In many cases, yes. Video-Assisted Thoracoscopic Surgery (VATS) uses small incisions and a camera, resulting in less pain and faster recovery. Dr. Khadkaban will evaluate your tumor's size and location to determine if you are a candidate for minimally invasive surgery.
