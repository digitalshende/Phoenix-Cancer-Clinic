Breast Cancer Care

Comprehensive Breast Oncology

A diagnosis of breast cancer can feel overwhelming. At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we are here to guide you through every step with clarity, compassion, and world-class medical expertise. Our approach is not just about removing the disease; it is about preserving your health, your confidence, and your quality of life.

Know the Signs

Early detection is the key to successful breast cancer treatment. We encourage you to perform regular self-examinations and consult a doctor if you notice any of the following:

  • A new lump in the breast or underarm
  • Thickening or swelling of part of the breast
  • Irritation or dimpling of breast skin
  • Redness or flaky skin in the nipple area
  • Pulling in of the nipple or pain in the nipple area
  • Any change in the size or the shape of the breast

AI-Assisted Screening

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we use advanced AI-assisted tools to analyze mammograms, ultrasounds, and pathology reports. This technology helps our team detect subtle abnormalities earlier and more accurately than traditional methods alone, ensuring that nothing is missed.

Our Surgical Procedures

Advanced surgeries tailored to your specific diagnosis

Breast Conservation Surgery

Also known as a lumpectomy, this procedure involves removing the tumor while preserving as much normal breast tissue as possible. It is often followed by radiation therapy and is the preferred choice for many early-stage breast cancers.

Oncoplastic Reconstruction

A specialized technique that combines cancer removal with plastic surgery principles. This approach ensures the best possible cosmetic outcome, maintaining the natural shape and appearance of the breast after tumor removal.

Modified Radical Mastectomy

In cases where the cancer is extensive, a mastectomy may be necessary. This involves the complete removal of the breast tissue and the surrounding lymph nodes, ensuring the highest level of oncological safety.

Our Approach

Our clinic provides a multidisciplinary approach, coordinating with medical oncologists and radiation oncologists to provide a complete treatment plan tailored to the patient's specific cancer type and stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Addressing your concerns

Will I lose my breast?

Not necessarily. Thanks to advances in Breast Conservation Surgery and Oncoplastic techniques, many women can keep their natural breast. Dr. Khadkaban will discuss the best surgical option based on the size and location of your tumor.

How long is the recovery after breast surgery?

Recovery varies depending on the type of surgery. A lumpectomy often requires 1-2 weeks of recovery, while a mastectomy may take 3-4 weeks. Our team provides comprehensive post-operative care to ensure you heal comfortably.

Will I need chemotherapy or radiation?

The need for chemotherapy or radiation depends on the stage of the cancer and the pathology report. Our multidisciplinary team will coordinate with specialists to ensure you receive the exact adjuvant therapies you need, and no more.
