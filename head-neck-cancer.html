Head & Neck Cancers

Thyroid & Throat Cancer Surgery

The head and neck region is one of the most complex areas of the human body, containing vital structures responsible for breathing, swallowing, speaking, and facial expressions. When cancer develops here, surgical removal requires extreme precision.

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, Dr. Khadkaban brings specialized expertise in head and neck oncology. Our goal is not only to remove the cancer completely but to preserve your vital functions and quality of life to the highest degree possible.

Signs, Symptoms & Risk Factors

Early detection is critical in the head and neck region

When to see a doctor:

  • A lump or swelling in the neck, thyroid, or jaw.
  • Persistent hoarseness or changes in your voice lasting more than two weeks.
  • Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia) or a feeling that food is sticking in your throat.
  • Persistent sore throat or ear pain.
  • Unexplained coughing or difficulty breathing.

Major Risk Factors:

  • Tobacco & Alcohol: The combination significantly increases the risk of throat and laryngeal cancers.
  • HPV Infection: Strongly linked to oropharyngeal cancers (tonsils and base of tongue).
  • Radiation Exposure: Previous radiation to the head and neck area increases thyroid cancer risk.
  • Family History: Genetic syndromes like MEN2 can cause medullary thyroid cancer.

Surgical Procedures Offered

Specialized surgeries for thyroid, salivary, and throat cancers

Thyroidectomy

The surgical removal of the thyroid gland for thyroid cancer. Depending on the size and type of tumor, Dr. Khadkaban may perform a partial (lobectomy) or total thyroidectomy. This procedure often utilizes advanced nerve monitoring to protect the vocal cords.

Parotidectomy

The surgical removal of the parotid gland (the largest salivary gland) for tumors. Because the facial nerve runs directly through this gland, the surgery requires extreme precision to remove the tumor while preserving your ability to smile, blink, and move your face.

Laryngectomy & Neck Surgery

Advanced procedures for laryngeal (voice box) cancers. In some cases, a partial or total removal of the voice box is necessary. This is often combined with a "Neck Dissection" to remove lymph nodes that may contain cancer cells.

Preserving Function & Voice

Head and neck surgeries require extreme precision to remove the cancer while preserving vital functions like swallowing, breathing, and speech. Dr. Khadkaban's expertise in this area ensures the best possible functional outcomes.

For patients who require a total laryngectomy, we work closely with speech therapists to provide voice restoration options, including tracheoesophageal puncture (TEP) or electrolarynx devices. Our multidisciplinary team ensures you are fully supported in adapting to changes in speech and swallowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Addressing your concerns about head and neck surgery

Will I lose my voice after throat cancer surgery?

Not always. Many laryngeal cancers can be treated with partial surgery or radiation therapy, preserving your voice. If a total laryngectomy (removal of the voice box) is necessary, we provide comprehensive voice rehabilitation options so you can learn to speak again using a prosthesis or an electronic device.

Will I need to take medication after a thyroidectomy?

If your entire thyroid gland is removed (total thyroidectomy), you will need to take daily thyroid hormone replacement medication (like Levothyroxine) for the rest of your life. This medication is safe, inexpensive, and simply replaces the hormone your body is no longer producing.

Will I have a scar on my neck?

Yes, a thyroidectomy or neck dissection typically leaves a scar. However, Dr. Khadkaban uses advanced surgical techniques, placing the incision in a natural skin fold on the neck to minimize visibility. The scar will fade significantly over time.
