Dr. Dhiraj S. Khadkaban is a distinguished name in the field of oncology, renowned for his expertise, compassion, and commitment to excellence.

Education and Training

Dr. Khadkaban completed his Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) from a prestigious institution, followed by a Master of Surgery (MS) specializing in surgical oncology. He further honed his skills and expertise through a Fellowship in Surgical Oncology, gaining advanced training in the diagnosis, treatment, and management of various complex cancers.

Professional Experience

With years of experience in the field of oncology, Dr. Khadkaban has established himself as a trusted leader and expert in surgical oncology. His dedication to advancing the field of cancer care is evident in his commitment to staying abreast of the latest advancements in oncology research and treatment modalities.

As a Consultant Surgical Oncologist, he specializes in the surgical management of cancer, employing the latest techniques and technologies to provide optimal outcomes for his patients. He works closely with a multidisciplinary team of oncologists, radiation therapists, nurses, and support staff to deliver comprehensive, personalized care tailored to each patient's unique needs.

Founder of Phoenix Cancer Clinic

Driven by a vision to provide world-class cancer care closer to home, Dr. Khadkaban founded Phoenix Cancer Clinic Satara. Under his leadership, the clinic has become a beacon of hope and healing for cancer patients in the Satara region, offering state-of-the-art facilities, advanced treatment options (including AI-assisted diagnosis), and compassionate support services.

Specializations

  • Laparoscopic Cancer Surgery (Colon, Stomach)
  • Laser Surgery for Oral Cancer
  • Complex Head & Neck Surgeries
  • Breast Conservation & Oncoplastic Surgery
  • Gastrointestinal & Hepatobiliary Surgeries

Compassionate Care

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic Satara, Dr. Khadkaban and his team are committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care that focuses not only on treating the disease but also on addressing the holistic needs of each individual. With empathy, understanding, and respect, they strive to empower patients and their families to navigate their cancer journey with dignity, courage, and hope.
