Advanced Treatments & Therapies

Comprehensive Oncology Services

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we believe that cancer treatment is not a one-size-fits-all approach. Every patient's cancer is unique, driven by different genetic mutations, located in different areas, and at different stages.

Under the guidance of Dr. Khadkaban, we utilize a multidisciplinary approach, combining advanced surgical oncology with systemic therapies. We coordinate with leading medical oncologists and radiation oncologists to ensure you receive the most effective, evidence-based treatment plan.

Systemic & Diagnostic Therapies

Targeting cancer at the cellular level

Chemotherapy

Administering powerful anti-cancer drugs to destroy cancer cells or stop their growth. We follow globally recognized protocols to ensure maximum efficacy while carefully managing side effects.

Immunotherapy

Boosting the body's immune system to recognize and fight cancer cells more effectively. This is a revolutionary treatment that trains your own body to act as a defense mechanism against the disease.

Targeted Therapy

Using drugs that specifically target the genetic mutations and proteins unique to certain cancer cells. This "precision medicine" approach minimizes damage to normal, healthy cells and reduces side effects.

Hormone Therapy

Used for cancers that are driven by hormones (like breast and prostate cancer). This therapy works by blocking the body's natural hormones or altering their levels to slow down or stop cancer growth.

Biopsy & Pathology

Accurate tissue sampling and analysis is the foundation of cancer treatment. We integrate digital pathology and AI to analyze your tissue samples for precise diagnosis and molecular profiling.

Laser Surgery

A minimally invasive surgery for oral cancers and other accessible tumors. Laser surgery offers exceptional precision, less bleeding, and significantly faster recovery times compared to traditional surgical methods.

How We Choose Your Treatment Plan

Personalized care based on science and your specific needs

1. Accurate Diagnosis & Staging

Using biopsy results and AI-assisted scans, we determine the exact type and stage of your cancer.

2. Biomarker Analysis

We analyze the genetic and molecular makeup of your tumor to see which targeted or immunotherapies will be most effective.

3. Multidisciplinary Board

Dr. Khadkaban collaborates with medical and radiation oncologists to finalize a comprehensive treatment sequence.

Specialized Surgical Procedures

Beyond systemic therapies, we specialize in advanced surgical interventions to physically remove cancer. Our surgical expertise includes Laparoscopic Cancer Surgery (Colon, Stomach), Laser Surgery for Oral Cancer, and complex Surgical Planning using AI-assisted imaging for maximum safety and precision.
