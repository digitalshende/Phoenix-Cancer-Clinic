Specialized Surgical Procedures

Expertise in Complex Cancer Surgeries

Surgical oncology is not just about removing a tumor; it is about removing the cancer completely while preserving your body's function, minimizing your recovery time, and restoring your quality of life.

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, Dr. Dhiraj Khadkaban brings advanced surgical techniques and years of specialized experience to the operating room. We offer minimally invasive options, advanced reconstructive surgeries, and utilize AI-driven planning to ensure the highest level of safety and precision.

Our Core Surgical Expertise

Advanced techniques for better outcomes

Laparoscopic Cancer Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery for colon, stomach, and other gastrointestinal cancers. Instead of a large abdominal incision, Dr. Khadkaban operates through a few tiny incisions using a high-definition camera and specialized precision instruments.

Key Benefits:

Less post-operative pain, smaller scars, reduced risk of infection, and a significantly faster return to normal activities.

Laser Surgery for Oral Cancer

Advanced laser technology is used to precisely remove oral tumors while preserving maximum healthy tissue. This is especially critical in the mouth and throat, where tissue preservation directly impacts your ability to speak and swallow.

Key Benefits:

Unmatched precision, less bleeding during surgery, reduced swelling, and improved functional and cosmetic outcomes.

AI-Assisted Surgical Planning

We don't enter the operating room blindly. Utilizing advanced AI-assisted imaging and 3D reconstruction, we meticulously map out the exact location, size, and relationship of the tumor to surrounding blood vessels and vital organs before surgery.

Key Benefits:

Maximum surgical safety, reduced operating time, complete tumor clearance, and minimized risk to healthy tissues.

Reconstructive Surgery

Post-cancer reconstruction is vital for restoring both appearance and function. We offer advanced procedures like Free Flap reconstruction (using tissue from other parts of the body to rebuild the face or mouth) and Oncoplastic breast surgery (combining cancer removal with cosmetic reconstruction).

Key Benefits:

Significantly improves psychological well-being, restores natural body contours, and helps you return to your daily life with confidence.

Commitment to Surgical Safety

Complex cancer surgeries require a highly skilled team and a strict adherence to safety protocols. At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we leave nothing to chance:

  • Pre-operative Optimization: We ensure your heart, lungs, and overall health are optimized before surgery.
  • Advanced Anesthesia: We work with highly experienced anesthesiologists to ensure your comfort and safety during the procedure.
  • Infection Control: We maintain the highest sterile standards in our operating environment to prevent post-surgical infections.

Surgical FAQs

Answers to common questions before surgery

Is laparoscopic surgery as effective as open surgery for cancer?

Yes. For many cancers, especially colon and stomach cancers, laparoscopic surgery is just as effective as open surgery in terms of removing the cancer and ensuring long-term survival. The main difference is the method of access, which offers the patient a much easier recovery.

How long will my scar be?

The goal of minimally invasive surgery is to minimize scarring. A laparoscopic surgery might leave 3 to 5 tiny scars (each about 1 cm long), whereas open surgery would require a much larger incision. For neck or breast surgeries, we use techniques that hide scars in natural skin folds or preserve the breast shape.

Do you provide pain management after surgery?

Absolutely. Managing your pain is a top priority. We use a combination of modern pain medications, nerve blocks, and minimally invasive techniques to ensure you are as comfortable as possible during your hospital stay and at home.
