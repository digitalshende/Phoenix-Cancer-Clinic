Oral & Mouth Cancer

Advanced Head & Neck Oncology

Oral cancer is highly prevalent in India, often linked to tobacco, gutkha, and areca nut consumption. Early detection and precise surgical intervention are critical for survival and maintaining a high quality of life.

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we offer advanced surgical options for mouth, tongue, and jaw cancers. By combining AI-assisted diagnostic imaging with Dr. Khadkaban's surgical expertise, we ensure accurate tumor mapping and effective removal while preserving as much healthy tissue as possible.

Signs, Symptoms & Risk Factors

Listen to your body. Early detection saves lives.

When to see a doctor:

  • A mouth ulcer or sore that doesn't heal within 2-3 weeks.
  • White or red patches inside the mouth (Leukoplakia/Erythroplakia).
  • A lump or thickening in the cheek, lip, or neck.
  • Difficulty chewing, swallowing, or moving the tongue/jaw.
  • Unexplained bleeding or numbness in the mouth.

Major Risk Factors:

  • Tobacco Use: Smoking, chewing tobacco, gutkha, and snuff are the leading causes.
  • Alcohol Consumption: Heavy and regular alcohol intake increases risk.
  • HPV Infection: Certain strains of the Human Papillomavirus.
  • Poor Oral Hygiene: Chronic irritation from sharp teeth or ill-fitting dentures.

Our Surgical Procedures

Tailored surgical interventions for head and neck cancers

Wide Local Excision

The primary surgical treatment for early-stage oral cancer. The surgeon removes the tumor along with a "margin" of healthy tissue around it to ensure all cancer cells are gone. In many cases, this can be performed using advanced laser technology.

Commando Surgery

A radical and complex surgery reserved for advanced oral cancer. It involves the en-bloc removal of the primary tumor, a portion of the jawbone (mandible), and the lymph nodes in the neck to prevent the cancer from spreading further.

Neck Dissection

Oral cancers frequently spread to the lymph nodes in the neck. This procedure involves the careful removal of these lymph nodes. It is often done simultaneously with the primary tumor removal to ensure comprehensive treatment.

Free Flap Reconstruction

After removing large tumors, reconstructing the oral cavity is vital for speaking and eating. This advanced microsurgery involves taking tissue (skin, muscle, or bone) from another part of the body (like the forearm or thigh) and connecting its blood vessels to the head and neck area.

The Laser Surgery Advantage

At Phoenix Cancer Clinic, we utilize advanced Laser Surgery for suitable oral cancers. This minimally invasive approach offers significant benefits over traditional scalpel surgery:


Less Bleeding

Lasers seal blood vessels as they cut.


Faster Recovery

Less swelling and quicker return to normal life.


Preserved Function

Precision helps maintain speech and swallowing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the road ahead

Will I be able to speak normally after surgery?

The ability to speak depends on the size and location of the tumor. For smaller tumors treated with laser, speech is often minimally affected. For larger tumors requiring reconstruction, speech therapy is provided to help you adapt and regain clear communication.

What is a Free Flap?

A free flap is a piece of tissue with its own blood supply taken from another part of your body (like your arm or leg) to rebuild the area where the cancer was removed. It is a highly specialized microsurgery that helps restore the shape and function of your mouth.

Do I have to stop chewing tobacco before surgery?

Yes, absolutely. Continued use of tobacco or gutkha severely impairs your body's ability to heal, increases the risk of surgical complications, and drastically increases the chance of the cancer returning. We provide support to help you quit.

Don't Ignore a Non-Healing Ulcer

If you or a loved one has symptoms of oral cancer, early intervention is key. Schedule a consultation with Dr. Khadkaban for a thorough evaluation.
